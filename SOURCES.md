Its SHA256 `0af66d4d24302b1ff9bb960ab189d41850603be1e8fd16edae29206fedfea058` matches the [November 12 build record](mnn-fpga/docs/benchmarks/fc1-capacity-2026-11-12/build-provenance.json).

The active demo includes both 21M MNN exports. The architecture comes from [Microsoft Cream](https://github.com/microsoft/Cream/tree/4a13c4091e78f9abd2160e7e01c02e48c1cf8fb9/TinyViT). The official [21M 224 checkpoint](https://github.com/wkcn/TinyViT-model-zoo/releases/download/checkpoints/tiny_vit_21m_22kto1k_distill.pth) matches the SHA256 in the original baseline. Calibration and host validation use [Imagenette2-160](https://s3.amazonaws.com/fast-ai-imageclas/imagenette2-160.tgz); the data manifest records original archive entries and hashes. The bundled NI82 image [matmul-soc-native-v4-90mhz.sof](hardware/bitstreams/ni82/matmul-soc-native-v4-90mhz.sof) matches the [upstream binary](https://github.com/PlutoPuppy/mnn-fpga/blob/41e25e13f792ee8caa94b27ee988ecc3c2fa6c94/build/matmul-soc-deploy/matmul-soc-native-v4-90mhz.sof). Its hash and prior graph's qualification reports are in mnn-fpga/docs/tinyvit-native-resident-offload.md.
